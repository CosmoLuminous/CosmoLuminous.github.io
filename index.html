About

Who I am

Researcher working at the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and medicine to develop interpretable, physiology-informed AI for healthcare in resource-constrained settings.

I develop constrained optimization methods that build domain knowledge directly into computational models, rather than treating them as black boxes. Together with clinicians at AIIMS-Delhi and AFMC (Armed Forces Medical College), Pune, I work on diagnostic tools designed to be transparent and usable where specialist care is scarce.

At IBM India Research Lab, I apply the same emphasis on reliability to enterprise AI, building LLM-based systems for modernizing legacy code in financial and healthcare domains, including work on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and AI agents.

I'm always happy to talk with researchers and clinicians working on similar problems.

Research Focus

What I work on

Three interlocking threads — physiology, clinical AI, and enterprise systems — that together define my research identity.

Physiology-Informed AI

Developing physiology-informed models for interpretable ECG analysis and holistic cardiac profiling from long-term recordings.

Enterprise AI Systems

Architecting spec-driven AI-workflows for legacy enterprise-scale system modernization, garnering skills in LLMs, RAG, and AI Agents.

Brain Stroke Diagnosis

Robust ML frameworks for early stroke diagnosis in resource-limited environments, addressing the 3rd leading cause of disability globally.
